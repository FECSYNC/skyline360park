            What to put on your page

            Type each item on its own line in Notes, Word, Google Docs, or any text app (not handwriting). Use the label, a colon, then your answer — see the example below. Then take one clear photo of the whole page. Sideways photos are okay. A second page is optional — if you add Extra page, it is also read and merged (handy for minors or overflow). Photos are only used to fill this form and are not stored.

            Copy this into Notes, Word, Google Docs, or any text app. Replace the sample values with your real details, then photograph the page. 

            First name: Daniel
Last name: Williams
Date of birth: 24-06-1996
Phone number: +1 202-555-0147
Street address: 123 Maple Street
City: Dallas
State / province: Texas
ZIP / postal code: 75201
Emergency contact name: Sarah Williams
Emergency phone: +1 202-555-0199

Minor 1 first name: Ana
Minor 1 last name: Williams
Minor 1 date of birth: 12-03-2014
Minor 2 first name: Leo
Minor 2 last name: Williams
Minor 2 date of birth: 05-11-2018

            Skip the Minor lines if you are signing for yourself only (“Just me”). Keep the same label wording so the scan can read each line.
