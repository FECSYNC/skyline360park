FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a waiver?

Yes. Guests must complete a waiver before participating. Adults 18+ sign their own waiver. Guests under 18 need a waiver signed by a parent, guardian, or accompanying adult.

Can I book a birthday party?

Absolutely. Skyline 360 Park offers customizable birthday packages with private spaces, attractions, dedicated hosts, food options, and decorations. Walk-in parties may be available depending on capacity.

Is Skyline 360 Park safe for all ages?

Your safety is our priority. Attractions are well maintained, regularly inspected, and supported by trained team members, clear guidelines, and assistance throughout the park.

Do you host groups and corporate events?

Yes. We welcome schools, churches, youth organizations, sports teams, community groups, and corporate team-building events. Our team can customize packages to match your goals.
